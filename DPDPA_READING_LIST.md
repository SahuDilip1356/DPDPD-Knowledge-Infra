## How to read the columns

**Stage** is the legal text that existed when the piece was written. It comes from the publication date, and it is the main check against stale analysis.

| Stage | Window | What the author could have been analysing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bill** | before 11 Aug 2023 | The 2023 Bill, before enactment |
| **Act** | 11 Aug 2023 – 2 Jan 2025 | The Act only. No Rules existed yet |
| **Draft** | 3 Jan – 12 Nov 2025 | The **draft** Rules. Some provisions changed before notification |
| **Final** | from 13 Nov 2025 | The notified Rules |

**Layer** follows the constitution's trust weights. A score is halved when the source has no coordinate hash.

| Layer | Weight | Unhashed | Used here for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L4** Expert Opinions | 0.5 | 0.25 | Law firms, legal academia, legal publishers, legal and policy press |
| **L5** Industry Guidance | 0.3 | 0.15 | Consultancies and compliance vendors |

⚠️ **Two judgment calls.** The constitution has no layer for the press, so I placed Supreme Court Observer and Medianama at L4 because their work is analytical and sourced. A stricter reading puts them at L6 Discussions (0.2). Also, the Supreme Court Observer reports on the court but is not the court: the filings and orders it tracks are **Layer 2** material and should be obtained separately.

**Date flags:** `†` batch-stamped (three DPDPA.com pages carry the same date) · `‡` malformed in source metadata · `◐` living page, where the date shown is page metadata · `—` no date published

## Corrections made to the source list

The original list had 50 numbered entries. It pointed to 43 distinct URLs.

* **9 duplicate entries removed.** The DPDPA.com Rules analysis appeared four times (#1, #17, #34, #46). Five other URLs appeared twice: privacy-policy guide #4/#24, Consent Manager #7/#22, FAQ #8/#26, EY #15/#27 and blog index #25/#33. The compensation article appeared twice under two URLs (#44/#48).
* **1 dead link.** The #48 URL (`…navigating-dps-blind-spot`) returns 404. The working URL is the #44 one (`…navigating-dpdps-blind-spot`).
* **1 wrong URL.** The primary Taxmann link in #20 (`analysis-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-act-and-rules`) loads a generic "Taxmann" shell page. The article lives at the citation URL, `analysis-indias-dpdp-act-and-rules`.
* **1 misattribution.** #49 was credited to "Lewis Silkin". The PDF is hosted on `lw.com` and credits **Latham & Watkins** throughout. "Lewis Silkin" does not appear in it.
* **3 draft-stage pieces were presented as analysis of the Rules.** Two January 2025 Medianama pieces (#29 children, #41 exemptions) and the NLS piece (#43) were written about the **draft** Rules, before notification on 13 Nov 2025. They are now marked **Draft**.
* **1 wrong title.** #16 Seqrite is titled "DPDP Rules Under the DPDP Act – Key Changes Explained", not "What Changed from the Draft?". The old title was taken from the URL slug.
* **Two site assessments had no evidence behind them.** The original assessed "DPDPA Shield" and "DPDPA Compliance Analyser" but cited no article from either site. There is nothing to verify, so they are not listed.
* **Unverified summaries dropped.** The original's per-entry descriptions were not checked against the articles' content, so they are omitted. Titles and dates are verified. Summaries are not.
